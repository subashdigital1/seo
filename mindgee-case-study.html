Site Audit Findings

6 Critical SEO/GEO/AEO Problems Found

The audit revealed compounding issues — each one suppressing organic growth. Every card below was a live problem on the site.

01 / Technical
⚡
Poor Core Web Vitals
Desktop performance score dropped to 31 with a critical Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) of 14.8s, causing poor user experience and reduced page speed performance.

📉
Low Keyword Rankings

03 / CTR
🖱
Low CTR & Poor Average Position
Search visibility generated 198K impressions, but CTR remained at just 0.7% with an average position of 50.7. Key pages lacked compelling titles, meta descriptions, and SERP optimisation.
🖱
GSC Performance Report
CTR: 0.7% | Position: 50.7
04 / GEO Visibility
🤖
Low AI Search Visibility
Brand visibility across AI search platforms was extremely limited, with low brand mentions, weak prompt coverage, and minimal presence in AI-generated recommendations compared to competitors.
🤖
GEO Audit Report

05 / Schema
🏷️
Limited Structured Data Coverage
Basic schema markup was implemented, but advanced structured data opportunities were missing. Service, FAQ, Review, and Local Business schemas were not fully utilized to maximize search visibility.

🔗
Low Domain Authority
The website had a Domain Authority of 15 with only 65 linking root domains and 296 ranking keywords, indicating a weak backlink profile and limited organic authority compared to competitors.
