Site Audit Findings

6 Critical SEO/GEO/AEO Problems Found

The audit revealed compounding issues — each one suppressing organic growth. Every card below was a live problem on the site.

01 / Technical
⚡
Poor Core Web Vitals
Desktop performance score dropped to 31 with a critical Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) of 14.8s, causing poor user experience and reduced page speed performance.
⚡
Core Web Vitals Audit
Performance Score 31 & LCP 14.8s
02 / Rankings
📉
Low Keyword Rankings
Only 6 keywords ranking in the Top 10 and 7 keywords in the Top 3. Overall visibility is just 3.46312%, indicating limited organic search presence and significant room for ranking improvements.
📉
SEMrush Position Tracking
Visibility 3.46312% · Top 3: 7 · Top 10: 6 · Top 20: 21
03 / CTR
🖱
Low CTR & Poor Average Position
Search visibility generated 198K impressions, but CTR remained at just 0.7% with an average position of 50.7. Key pages lacked compelling titles, meta descriptions, and SERP optimisation.
🖱
GSC Performance Report
CTR: 0.7% | Position: 50.7
04 / GEO Visibility
🤖
Low AI Search Visibility
Brand visibility across AI search platforms was extremely limited, with low brand mentions, weak prompt coverage, and minimal presence in AI-generated recommendations compared to competitors.
🤖
GEO Audit Report
Low AI Search Visibility
05 / Schema
🏷️
Limited Structured Data Coverage
Basic schema markup was implemented, but advanced structured data opportunities were missing. Service, FAQ, Review, and Local Business schemas were not fully utilized to maximize search visibility.
🏷️
Schema Audit
Limited Structured Data Coverage
06 / Authority
🔗
Low Domain Authority
The website had a Domain Authority of 15 with only 65 linking root domains and 296 ranking keywords, indicating a weak backlink profile and limited organic authority compared to competitors.
🔗
Authority Audit
DA 15 • 65 Referring Domains
Execution Strategy

6 Pillars of SEO Execution

Each pillar targeted a specific weakness from the audit.

01 / Technical
⚡
Page Speed & GEO Optimization
Slow loading pages and weak crawl efficiency affecting GEO visibility
Improved Core Web Vitals by optimizing LCP, CLS and page rendering speed
Compressed images, enabled lazy loading and reduced page weight
Fixed crawl barriers and improved internal linking for better discovery
Optimized site architecture to help search engines understand content
Enhanced crawlability and entity signals to strengthen GEO visibility
Impact: Faster Page Speed · Better Crawlability · Improved GEO Visibility
⚡
Page Speed & GEO Strategy
Faster Crawling & Visibility
02 / Content
📝
Content & AEO Optimization
Thin, generic pages with no direct answers for AI Overviews or voice search
Rewrote core service pages around high-intent, localized search queries
Added FAQ blocks structured to directly answer common user questions
Restructured headings and content hierarchy for featured-snippet capture
Published supporting blog content to build topical depth and authority
Optimized copy for extractability by AI answer engines and chat assistants
Impact: Higher Rankings · Snippet Wins · AEO-Ready Content
📝
Content & AEO Strategy
Answer-Ready Content
03 / On-Page
🖱
Meta & SERP Optimization
Weak titles and meta descriptions suppressing CTR despite decent impressions
Rewrote title tags and meta descriptions across priority landing pages
Added compelling, benefit-led copy to improve SERP click-through
Implemented breadcrumb and rich-result-friendly on-page markup
A/B tested meta variants against real GSC impression data
Impact: CTR 0.7% → 1.4%+ · Better SERP Real Estate
🖱
Meta & SERP Strategy
Higher Click-Through Rate
04 / GEO
🤖
Generative Engine Optimization
Near-zero brand visibility inside AI Overviews and chat-based search
Structured content to be citation-friendly for AI answer engines
Built entity clarity around brand, services and location signals
Published authoritative, fact-dense pages AI models prefer to cite
Monitored brand mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews
Impact: New AI-Search Mentions · Stronger Entity Presence
🤖
GEO Strategy
AI Search Visibility
05 / Schema
🏷️
Structured Data Expansion
Missing Service, FAQ, Review and Local Business schema left rich results on the table
Implemented FAQ, Service and Local Business schema site-wide
Added Review and AggregateRating markup on relevant pages
Validated all markup against Google's Rich Results and Schema.org tests
Layered breadcrumb schema to strengthen SERP navigation cues
Impact: Rich Result Eligibility · Cleaner Entity Signals
🏷️
Schema Strategy
Full Structured Data Coverage
06 / Authority
🔗
Link Building & Authority Growth
Weak backlink profile with only 65 referring domains behind DA-40+ competitors
Ran targeted outreach for relevant, high-authority backlinks
Secured guest posts and directory listings in the marketing/tech niche
Built digital PR angles around client case studies and data
Disavowed toxic links dragging down the domain's trust signals
Impact: DA 15 → 19+ · Stronger Backlink Profile
🔗
Authority Strategy
Higher Domain Trust
